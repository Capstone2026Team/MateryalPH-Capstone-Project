# MateryalPH Capstone Privacy Notice

Version 2 · Environment: CAPSTONE/TEST · Effective 14 September 2026

This notice describes the MateryalPH capstone system. It is test content and is not represented as reviewed, certified, endorsed, or issued by a lawyer, the National Privacy Commission, another government agency, a regulator, or a service provider.

## Information handled by the system

Depending on a person's role and activity, MateryalPH may handle account and profile information; contact information; Buyer information; Vendor business and staff-account information; verification, registration, tax, and compliance evidence; address and location information; orders and transaction references; quotations and project-procurement records; messages and communications; fulfillment evidence; invoice and document records; security and audit events; device and session information used by the existing system; and payment-provider references.

MateryalPH uses this information for account registration and authentication, marketplace operation, Vendor verification, order and procurement processing, fulfillment, payment coordination, compliance review, fraud and security prevention, dispute resolution, support, notifications, audit and accountability, permitted analytics, and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

## Access and disclosure

Access is limited by role and purpose. Authorized information may be processed by infrastructure and hosting providers, the payment provider, the public-media provider, email and notification services, mapping or location providers, and other approved processors needed to operate the system. Information may also be disclosed when required for an applicable legal obligation, rights-protection process, or authorized dispute workflow.

Private Vendor information is not public marketplace information. This includes DTI, SEC, LGU, BIR, TIN, and tax evidence; private contacts; payout or payment credentials; authentication data; and private compliance evidence. Those records must not be placed in the normal public-media channel.

Information deliberately configured and approved for a public Vendor Store Profile may be visible to Buyers. Examples are the store name, approved logo and banner, public description, selected public business contact details, public listings, fulfillment methods, eligible compliance indicators, and public ratings or badges.

## Retention and requests

Records are kept for the applicable operational, security, dispute, financial, legal, regulatory, and audit purpose. They may be kept longer when a lawful requirement or legal hold applies. MateryalPH does not apply one universal deletion period to every category.

Subject to applicable limits, a person may ask about access, correction, objection or restriction, deletion or erasure, portability, or a privacy complaint. A deletion request may be limited when the record must remain for a lawful retention, security, financial, dispute, or audit purpose. ## Security

MateryalPH applies role-based access, authentication safeguards, private storage for sensitive evidence, short-lived authorized access, integrity checks, audit records, and operational security controls appropriate to this capstone system. No system can promise absolute security; suspected unauthorized access should be reported promptly through the configured support or privacy channel.
