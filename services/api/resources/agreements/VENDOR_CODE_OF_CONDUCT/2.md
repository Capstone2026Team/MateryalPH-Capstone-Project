# Vendor Code of Conduct — Capstone/Test

Version 2 · Environment: CAPSTONE/TEST · Effective 14 September 2026

This Code accompanies the MateryalPH Capstone Terms of Service. It is test content and is not a government or external-provider certification.

A Vendor must provide accurate business, contact, store, product, listing, fulfillment, tax, and compliance information and must submit only genuine evidence. The Vendor remains responsible for the products it sells, its authorized staff, fulfillment of accepted orders, legally required invoices and receipts, and applicable consumer, product, business, tax, and regulatory duties.

Credential sharing, fraud, forged evidence, misleading information, unlawful or prohibited products, unauthorized access, interference with security controls, and platform misuse are prohibited. MateryalPH may document, restrict, suspend, or remove access through the approved workflow. A platform verification indicator records a MateryalPH review outcome and does not create government certification or regulatory approval. Prior decisions, acceptance evidence, and required audit history remain retained and versioned.
