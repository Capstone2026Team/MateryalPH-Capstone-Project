# Vendor 2% Commission Terms — Capstone/Test

Version 1 · Environment: CAPSTONE/TEST · Effective 14 September 2026

These Terms implement the existing FIN-03 capstone policy. They are test content. They are not represented as legal, tax, regulatory, BIR, or Xendit approval, and TEST or demo records are not production payment or tax proof.

MateryalPH charges the Vendor a 2% platform commission on the completed, non-refunded materials value after discounts and excluding included Vendor VAT. The approved base excludes delivery, Buyer processing charges, Vendor VAT, NRPC deposits, cancelled-order value, retained cancellation NRPC, and other amounts excluded by FIN-03. The commission applies under the existing completed-order policy and is billed monthly. Cancellation and partial-refund credits follow the existing FIN rules and original affected amounts.

The commission is Vendor-paid. It is not added to Buyer checkout as a MateryalPH commission charge, does not change Buyer totals, and is not automatically deducted from the Buyer's payment to the Vendor. MateryalPH does not use Xendit Split Payments for this commission, and Xendit does not automatically send 2% of a Buyer payment to MateryalPH. The Vendor pays an issued positive statement through the approved TEST billing flow; no subscription or listing fee is created by these Terms.

Any VAT treatment of MateryalPH's service fee follows the approved finance design and the configured teaching profile. A TEST or demo classification is not a conclusion about the production operator's registration or tax treatment.

The Vendor Owner must accept the current commission version. A Store Manager may view or prepare only what the approved role permits and cannot attest, accept, or pay in the Owner's place. These Terms do not amend FIN-01 through FIN-12.
