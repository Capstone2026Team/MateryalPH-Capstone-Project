# Non-Recoverable Preparation Cost (NRPC) Terms — CAPSTONE/TEST

This version is labelled CAPSTONE/TEST content for the MateryalPH project demonstration. It is not a production legal, regulatory or payment-provider approval.

1. **What an NRPC is.** A Vendor may identify part of the agreed order value as a Non-Recoverable Preparation Cost for actual irreversible preparation of specific order lines, such as cutting, mixing, customization or a special order. An NRPC is part of the existing order value. It is never added on top of the agreed materials and delivery total, and it is not a cancellation fee or a second sale.
2. **What you see before accepting.** Before you pay or the Vendor prepares anything, you see the NRPC amount, the Vendor's reason, the affected order lines and the amount assigned to each, and this Terms version. The NRPC cannot exceed the payable value of the lines it covers. MateryalPH sets no other platform cap.
3. **Your decision.** You may accept or reject the NRPC. Rejecting it cancels the order request and releases the reserved stock. No payment or preparation starts until you accept. Your acceptance is recorded with this Terms version and the time of acceptance.
4. **Raising a concern.** You may flag an NRPC as disproportionate. A flag creates a review record for MateryalPH staff. It does not accept, reject or change the NRPC on its own, and your acceptance and your concern are kept as separate records. A review may lead to removal, reduction or another remedy through the dispute process.
5. **Payment.** For a fully online order, the NRPC is identified within the amount you pay. For Cash on Delivery or In-Store Payment, the accepted NRPC is collected first as a separate online assurance payment and credited against the balance you pay the Vendor. A processing fee on that assurance payment is not credited as materials value. An unpaid assurance payment expires after 45 minutes and the reserved stock is released.
6. **When the NRPC may be kept.** The Vendor may keep an accepted NRPC only when you cancel an eligible order during preparation (PROCESSING) and the Vendor can substantiate the preparation. The Vendor forfeits the NRPC when the Vendor cancels. An NRPC never limits your remedies for defective, incorrect, unsafe, misrepresented or otherwise nonconforming goods, or for a Vendor-caused failure.
7. **Auto-accept.** An order carrying an NRPC is always reviewed manually by the Vendor and is never confirmed automatically.

A later version of these Terms does not change an NRPC you already accepted under this version.
